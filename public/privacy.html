Judah TCG Books — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Judah TCG Books is a small family bookkeeping app for tracking Pokémon card purchases and sales. This page says plainly what it does with information.

What the app collects

Sign-in information. If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address from Google so it can show your own books to you. The app never sees or stores your Google password.

Your bookkeeping entries. The products, purchases, sales, and notes you type into the app are stored so the app can do its math and show your history.

Where it is stored

Entries are stored in a cloud database (Supabase) tied to your sign-in. Each signed-in account can only see its own books.

What the app does not do

It does not sell information, show ads, or share your information with anyone for marketing. It does not track you across other sites.

Service providers

The app relies on Google (sign-in), Supabase (database and sign-in service), and GitHub Pages (hosting the app itself). If you use the optional price lookup, product names are sent to the TCG API price service.

Deleting your information

To have your account and books deleted, contact the app owner at jen@integrityrealtystl.com and they will be removed.

Contact
